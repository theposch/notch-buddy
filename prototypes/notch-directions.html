Notch Buddy

Five ways the notch could look

Every screen below is live. Hover a closed notch to open it, press Allow, switch the scene. The newest comes first: Quiet and Signal, combined. Today's design and the earlier directions follow, so you can compare. All of them are built only from what a Mac already does: system type, system colours, hairlines, capsule buttons, Live Activities.

Direction 04 · Quiet + Signal

Quiet Signal

Calm until something needs you. Quiet's list is the everyday view; Signal steps in only for the session that is stuck.

  • From Signal: closed, a yellow "Needs you" pill. Otherwise Quiet's dots, one per session
  • From Signal: the stuck session opens as a card with the whole command and all three answers
  • From Quiet: every other session is one calm line, with model and account on hover
  • From Quiet: usage as a small ring per account with the percentage beside it; hover for the account's name and reset time
  • New: you choose what a row shows. Branch, tests, pull request and the rest come back on hover; the settings sheet below is live
Direction 00

Today

The notch as it ships in version 0.2. Everything it knows is on screen at once.

  • Two tags, a status, an activity and a meter on every row
  • State is told by the colour of one word
  • Usage is two numbers beside the camera
Direction 01

Quiet

A plain list. One line per session, with everything else one hover away.

  • A name and one line per session; model and account appear on hover
  • A ring and a number for usage; a ring alone for how full a conversation is
  • A finished session shows what Claude said last
  • Closed, the notch shows one dot per session
Direction 02

Signal

Sorted by what needs you. The session that is stuck becomes a notification you can answer; the rest stay small.

  • Closed, a yellow pill says "Needs you", like the Mac's own menu bar indicators
  • Grouped the way Notification Center groups: Needs you, Working, Done
  • The request shows the whole command and all three answers
Direction 03

Live

One session at a time, the way a Live Activity or Now Playing shows one thing well. Clawd gets room to perform.

  • The lead session at four times the size, with progress through Claude's to-do list
  • Allow and Deny as full-width buttons
  • Other sessions wait as chips; click one to bring it forward
  • Closed, the notch shows a running timer
These mix.

Quiet Signal at the top is one mix. Others are possible: Quiet's rows could sit under Live's lead session, and the yellow pill works on any closed notch.

What is real here.

The sessions, commands and numbers are examples. Clawd is the real one: the app's own drawing engine and all its animations. The colours, type sizes and fills are Apple's system values for dark appearance.
