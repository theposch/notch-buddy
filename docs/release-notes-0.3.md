# Notch Buddy 0.3

A new look for the notch, and Settings that look like they belong on a Mac.

## New

- **A calmer notch.** Each session is one quiet row: Clawd, a name, and one line saying what Claude is doing. Hover a row for the rest.
- **A card when Claude needs you.** The session that is waiting on you opens as a card with the whole command and three answers: Deny, Always allow, Allow.
- **A yellow pill on the closed notch.** When Claude needs you, the closed notch says so in a yellow pill, like the Mac's own menu bar indicators.
- **Usage as rings.** Each account shows a small ring in its colour and the percentage left. Hover for whose it is, when it resets, and the week.
- **You choose what a row shows.** In Settings > Sessions, set each detail (model, account, branch, tests, pull request, cost and more) to At a glance, On hover or Off. Or pick a preset: Minimal, Balanced, Everything.
- **What Claude said last.** A finished session shows how Claude's last answer began, so you know whether to go and read it.
- **A new Settings window.** A sidebar with eight sections. Look, Sessions, Messages and Shortcuts open with a live piece of the notch, so you see what a choice does as you make it.
- **Open the app to open Settings.** Opening Notch Buddy again while it's running now shows its settings.

## Changed

- Cost is off by default. Turn it on in Settings > Sessions.
- The weekly usage and the reset time moved from the top of the notch to hover.
- Status colours are now the Mac's own yellow, green and red.

## Good to know

- From 0.2, click **Update** in the notch or in Settings > General. From 0.1 or 0.1.1, download this version by hand once.
- This copy isn't signed with a Developer ID, so macOS asks again for Keychain access after an update. Click the **Allow usage** button in the notch and choose "Always Allow".
