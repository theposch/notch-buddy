Notch Buddy

Claude Code status, but cuter. See every session from your Mac's notch, and answer Claude without leaving what you're doing.

Hover the notch.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

Free and open source. Version 0.4, macOS 14 or later.

One calm line per session

Each session gets a row: Clawd, a name, and what Claude is doing right now.

Hover a row for the rest. The branch, the lines changed, the tests, the pull request. A finished session shows how Claude's answer began, so you know whether to go and read it.

A card when Claude needs you

The session that is waiting on you opens as a card. It shows the whole command and three answers: Deny, Always allow, Allow.

Closed, the notch says so in a yellow pill. Or skip the notch and press ⌥⌘Y from any app.

Usage you can read at a glance

A ring and a number for each Claude account, at the top of the notch. Hover for when it resets and how the week is going.

When you run low, Clawd starts to sweat. When you run out, he naps until it resets.

WorkResets 3:50 PMWeek: 61% left

You choose what a row shows

Model, account, branch, tests, pull request, cost. Put each one on the row, on hover, or nowhere.

Or pick a preset and get on with your day. Try them here.

Settings that look like a Mac

Eight sections in a sidebar. Look, Sessions, Messages and Shortcuts each open with a live piece of the notch, so you see what a choice does as you make it.

Open Notch Buddy again while it's running and Settings appears.

Updates in one click

When a new version is out, an Update button appears in the notch. One click downloads it, checks it, installs it and restarts.

Nothing is downloaded until you click.

Get it

Free, and open source under the MIT License. Nothing leaves your Mac unless you switch on reading usage from your Claude login.

Download for Mac Help
  1. Download it and unzip it.Drag Notch Buddy into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it.macOS asks first, because the app isn't signed by Apple yet. See below.
  3. Click Connect to Claude Code.That's it. Start a session and watch the notch.

The first time you open it: macOS says it can't check the app. Go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway. On macOS 14 you can also right-click the app and choose Open. After that it opens normally.
